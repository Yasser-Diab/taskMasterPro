Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

TaskMaster Pro helps you manage tasks, notes, roadmaps, reminders, focus sessions, browser workspaces, and optional health-related productivity signals across Windows and Android.

Data We Collect

We store your account email, profile name, username, avatar path, language, sex when provided, onboarding status, task domains, tasks, quick notes, task notes, roadmaps, reminders, schedules, Pomodoro sessions, activity intervals, device metadata, synced settings, diagnostic logs, and optional browser workspace metadata when enabled.

Health data is optional and requires platform permission. When enabled, TaskMaster Pro may read steps, exercise, distance, heart rate, sleep, active calories, and detected data sources through Health Connect or another implemented health provider.

Passwords and Browser Data

Account passwords are handled by the authentication provider and must never be stored as plain text by TaskMaster Pro. Website passwords, when enabled, are intended to sync as encrypted vault items per user, not readable Supabase rows. Browser history, tab URLs, page titles, and activity tracking are controllable in Settings.

How We Use Data

We use your data to authenticate you, synchronize your workspace, schedule reminders, restore sessions, calculate progress, provide coaching, detect active or idle time, and improve reliability. We do not use health or browser data for advertising.

Third Parties

TaskMaster Pro uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and realtime synchronization. Google authentication may be used when you choose Google sign-in. GitHub hosts public release downloads and this website.

Retention and Deletion

Data is retained while your account remains active or until you delete it. You can request account and data deletion from inside the app or by contacting the maintainer. Deleted records may use tombstones briefly so synchronization can complete.

Security

The app uses row-level security, authenticated user ownership, local device storage, and platform credential storage where supported. No system can be guaranteed perfect, so keep your device secure and change exposed passwords promptly.

Children

TaskMaster Pro is not intended for children under 13.

# DayVector Brand Guidelines — Exact Approved Artwork

The approved source image is the authority. Do not redraw, rotate, skew, stretch, simplify, or replace its symbol geometry. Primary assets in this package are direct crops/scales from that image. One-color files are silhouette-only technical variants for tray, notification, monochrome, and accessibility use. Keep at least 12.5% clear space around the mark. Use symbol-only assets for tiny surfaces. Do not add “Pro”.
